As part of the initial development phase of our AI Football Analysis Platform, we are establishing the Player Database and Skill Evaluation Framework.

This will be the foundation for the system's player intelligence capabilities, allowing the AI to evaluate individual player attributes, understand positional responsibilities, and eventually generate tactical recommendations based on the team's available personnel.

Before implementing this module, I would appreciate your input on the following areas.

1. Player Information and Physical Attributes
We intend to create an individual profile for every player, initially including:

First Name / Last Name

Age / Date of Birth

Height

Weight

Primary Position

Secondary Position(s)

Question: What additional player information or measurable attributes would you consider relevant for coaching, player evaluation, and tactical planning?

For example, we could include 40-yard dash time, 10-yard split, vertical jump, wingspan, strength measurements, dominant hand, or any other metrics you consider valuable.

The database will support custom attributes, so teams will be able to define and maintain their own evaluation criteria.

2. Position Classification
Each player will be assigned one or more positions. These classifications will determine which position-specific skills and competencies are available for evaluation.

Question: Could you provide your preferred complete list of offensive, defensive, and special teams positions, including any distinctions you consider important?

For example:

Defensive End (DE)

Defensive Tackle (DT)

Linebacker (LB)

Running Back (RB)

Tight End (TE)

Wide Receiver (WR)

Please specify whether you would prefer broader positional groups or more detailed classifications, such as MIKE/WILL/SAM, Nose Tackle/Defensive Tackle, Slot Receiver/Outside Receiver, etc.

We would like to establish a comprehensive initial positional structure based on your coaching methodology.

3. Position-Specific Skills and Competencies
This is the most important part of the initial database design.

Each position will have a defined set of individual skills that coaches can evaluate on a 0–100 scale.

For example:

Defensive End (DE)

Rip Move

Swim Move

Bull Rush

Edge Setting

Block Shedding

Run Defense

Pursuit

Tackling

Zone Coverage

Running Back (RB)

Vision

Acceleration

Agility

Inside Running

Outside Running

Pass Protection

Route Running

Ball Security

Receiving

These are preliminary examples rather than finalized skill classifications.

Question: Could you prepare an initial list of the skills and competencies that should be evaluated for each position?

Ideally, we would like to identify all relevant skills under every positional category, including technical, tactical, physical, and position-specific competencies.

At this stage, we do not need the final or perfect classification. The objective is to establish a strong initial framework that we can refine through discussions with additional experienced coaches and positional specialists.

The system will be fully customizable at the team level. Teams will be able to add, remove, rename, and modify position-specific skills according to their own coaching philosophy and evaluation methodology.

4. Initial Implementation and Next Steps
Our immediate development priority is the Player Information and Skill Evaluation Module.

Once we establish the initial player attributes, position classifications, and associated skill lists, I will provide you with access to a platform where you can begin entering player profiles and assigning individual skill ratings.

I expect to have an initial version of this interface available next week.

For now, I would primarily need your input on three deliverables:

Player Attributes: The information and measurable metrics we should collect for each athlete.

Position Structure: Your preferred complete list of football positions and positional subcategories.

Skill Framework: The technical, tactical, and physical skills that should be evaluated under each position.

This initial dataset will serve as the foundation for future player comparison, personnel evaluation, and AI-driven tactical analysis.

Thank you for your contribution to establishing the football intelligence framework of the platform.
